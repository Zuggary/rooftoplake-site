The problem

You already know what's broken.

It's what happens when every job gets its own rented tool, and every tool keeps its own copy of your business.

Five reports, five different numbers

The DMS says one thing, the CRM another, the spreadsheet a third. Monday's meeting is spent arguing about whose number is right.

With the lake: one number, one definition, every store.

A new subscription for every job

Each problem gets another vendor, another login and another monthly bill per store. The stack grows. The answers don't get clearer.

With the factory: build the small tools yourself, inside the assistant your team already uses. No new login.

Your data lives in other people's systems

It's your customers, your deals and your service history, but getting a full copy out is slow, limited, or costs extra.

With the lake: your own copy, in your own account, in open formats.

Your people already use AI, and nobody has checked it

Staff paste work into personal AI accounts to get through the day, sometimes with customer details in it. Under the FTC Safeguards Rule, where that data goes is your responsibility.

With us: business accounts, house rules, a record of use, and a readiness pack for your attorney.

Straight answers

Questions a careful dealer would ask.

Is this live? Who's using it?

No one yet. RooftopLake is pre-launch. We're choosing a small number of design-partner groups to build the first version with. There are no customer logos on this page because we haven't earned any.

Do I have to change my DMS or CRM?

No. The lake copies data out of the systems you already run. Your people keep working in the same screens.

How do you get the data out of my systems?

Through the access routes your vendors offer to dealers and their approved partners. Some are simple, some need a contract, and some carry a fee from the vendor. This is the slowest part of any project like this, and we'll tell you where each of your systems stands before you commit to anything.

Reading data is the easy half. Letting an agent write into a system needs that vendor's agreement. Until it's in place, agents prepare the work and a person enters it.

Who owns the data and the agents?

You do. The lake sits in a cloud data account in your group's name. The job sheets, definitions and agents built for you are yours to keep.

Are we even allowed to use AI on our vendor data?

Often yes, with conditions, and it depends on your contracts and your state. Your agreements with your DMS, CRM and manufacturer each say something about who may touch the data and how. Part of our onboarding is a readiness pack: your documents, the clauses that matter and a short list of questions, prepared for your attorney to rule on. We don't give legal advice.

Which AI does it use?

Your choice. The door is an MCP server. MCP is the open standard that the major AI assistants use to reach data and tools, so you aren't tied to one AI company, and you can switch as they improve.

Is it safe to let AI near customer data?

Only with rules. Each person and each agent sees just what their role allows, every request is logged, each agent has a spending cap and an off switch, and nothing reaches a customer without a named person's approval.

We hold no security certifications yet and won't pretend to. The Safeguards Rule makes you responsible for checking vendors like us, so before any data moves you get our security plan and contract terms in writing. The first phase uses no credit applications or Social Security numbers, the lake sits in your account, and you can cut off our access at any time.

Is this about replacing my people?

No. It's about what they spend their day on. Agents take the looking-up, matching and re-keying. Your people keep the customer, the judgment and the sign-off. Staffing decisions stay with you.

We don't have developers. Can we really build our own tools?

Yes. You describe the job in plain English and decide what good looks like. We build it with you at first. The factory gives every agent the same tests and the same sign-off, so your own operations people can safely do more of it over time.

What does it cost?

We haven't published pricing. Design partners get a flat founding price agreed in writing up front. Ask and we'll tell you straight.
